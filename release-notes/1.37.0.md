# TPS Global Context Menu 1.37.0

## Intent

Make every GCM context menu reflect the item that was actually clicked, remove dead or duplicate actions, and prevent long configured values from widening menus and modals unpredictably.

## Changes

- Task, bullet, and heading menus are now exact-line menus. They no longer append actions for the containing or linked source note, eliminating duplicate Delete entries and unsupported note conversion, archive, attachment, and time-tracking actions.
- Whole-note/file menus omit read-only recurrence, generated timestamp/identity fields, and generic Parent aliases. Parent relationships remain available once through **Link to Parent** and **Link Children**.
- TPS Notebook Navigator owns its native title/rename, tags, and delete actions; GCM adds only its distinct configured properties and file workflows.
- All GCM-involved menus share a 25-character visible label boundary. Full labels remain available as native hover titles and accessibility labels. GCM modal content also wraps inside its configured and viewport width.

## Compatibility and migration

- Backward-compatible minor feature; minimum supported Obsidian remains 1.10.0.
- No setting, note, Base, API, or stored-data migration is required.
- Removing a menu entry does not remove the property from inline/stacked displays or literal Markdown. Recurrence remains editable through its supported task recurrence workflow, and generated fields remain owned by their creation/update services.

## Validation

- The full declared suite passed: 1,113 tests, including the new presentation, task-line ownership, TPS List ownership, menu-builder filtering, line-entity, and TPS Notebook Navigator bridge regressions.
- TypeScript validation and the mandatory separate production build passed.
- The final source artifacts are byte-identical to the deployed `tps-global-context-menu` test-vault runtime.
- Test-vault QA used Obsidian 1.13.7 and confirmed the final plugin loaded in `Obsidian Plugin Test Vault`; task-source navigation and task editing remained operational without saving fixture changes. Exact menu ownership, recursive submenu constraints, accessible full labels, and modal-width behavior are also covered by executable source/runtime regressions.

## Known limitations

- Obsidian and third-party hosts may add their own native actions outside GCM's extension section. GCM constrains only menus it builds or participates in and does not globally restyle unrelated menus.
- The 25-character boundary is visual; full values remain available on hover/accessibility and in the corresponding editor.
