# TPS Global Context Menu 1.34.4

TPS Global Context Menu 1.34.4 fixes linked-context mount recovery so Reading View remains freely scrollable.

## Linked-context scroll stability

- A panel detached by Obsidian's Reading View or Live Preview DOM replacement is recovered only after 240 ms of scroll inactivity.
- The existing rendered panel is reused when its request identity is unchanged; GCM does not rescan or rerender its cards merely because Obsidian replaced the mount.
- GCM snapshots and restores the exact scroll offset around both panel reattachment and fresh-render commits.
- Top placement fails closed while Reading View has virtualized the title away, preventing a top panel from being inserted beside a mid-note virtualized section.
- Recovery still uses bounded retries and releases its MutationObserver, passive scroll listener, and pending timer with the owning view.

## Compatibility and known limitations

- Backward-compatible patch with no new setting, public API, or note schema.
- Linked-context placement, source ordering, task controls, and completed-task visibility are unchanged.
- A top-placed panel can remain temporarily detached while the note is scrolled far enough that Obsidian has removed the title from the rendered DOM. It recovers when the top mount becomes available; this is intentional to preserve scroll stability.
- Minimum supported Obsidian remains 1.10.0.
