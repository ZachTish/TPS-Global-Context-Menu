# TPS Global Context Menu 7.3.3 — Keep synced completions completed

Backward-compatible patch; minimum Obsidian 1.10.0.

## Fix

The external checklist guard was an active writer attached to ordinary metadata updates. After another writer saved a completed note with unchecked body items, it restored the prior status and removed `completedDate`, then waited for a checklist modal. A synced completion could therefore be reopened locally and published as another overdue reminder. A bounded live diagnostic identified this exact writer: the saved completion was removed by the metadata guard about 260 ms later. No note contents or identifiers are included in this release.

The post-save guard, its status history, and its timers are removed. Metadata refreshes now preserve authored completion. GCM's explicit status actions retain their existing pre-save checklist and parent checks, cancellation, and guarded write behavior. No preference is reset or disabled, and no automatic note repair, deduplication, retry, migration, or new state is added. The 7.3.2 source/rename race fix remains intact. Controller 3.1.2 separately confirms successful completion and publishes current-source reminder schedules.

## Validation

- The enabled-checklist regression fails against 7.3.2: its passive observer writes once, deletes saved completion fields, scans twice, and opens a modal. The fixed observer preserves the exact authored frontmatter and body with zero writes, scans, or prompts.
- 129 focused checks pass, including explicit checklist cancellation/Ignore and guarded status writes. New event cases cover stale/repeated metadata, startup, custom keys, status aliases, native/legacy/mobile modes, newer reopening, rename, file replacement, and unload.
- The final declared `npm test` passes 1,541 checks; the separately invoked supplementary `prepretest` script passes 209 checks. Both have zero failures, skips, or cancellations. These are test executions, not a claim of 1,750 unique cases. TypeScript and production bundling pass.
- A separate stable build deployed only the changed runtime artifacts into the isolated Test vault. The official Obsidian CLI reloaded and confirmed GCM 7.3.3 with Controller 3.1.2.
- Installed verification used the real Controller projection and a synthetic overdue note with an unchecked body item, keeping checklist checking enabled in memory. Six projected triggers became zero immediately after externally authored completion and remained zero after metadata settled and after an unrelated edit. Exact completion fields/body and one-file identity were preserved; the fixture was archived.
- A second installed test called the real Controller completion route into GCM and observed six triggers become zero immediately and after settlement. The configured filename service retained one note and preserved its body. A paused asynchronous property writer was then rejected after a newer completion, with one mutator execution, the newer date/body intact, and zero remaining triggers. Background automation stayed disabled in the Test vault, so this QA invoked the real filename owner directly rather than claiming its background scheduling was enabled. Both the completed fixture and an earlier assertion-only fixture were inspected and archived.
- Test-vault GCM and Controller settings remained byte-identical. No real delivery, pairing, production settings change, or phone installation was part of this QA.

Tested in the isolated vault and ready for the user's BRAT pull. Direct production installation and receiving-device cancellation are separate checks and are not implied by these results. Explicit GCM completion still honors the user's pre-save confirmation settings; this patch removes passive rollback, not those user-invoked prompts.
